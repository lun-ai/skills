---
name: explain-concept
description: "Explain a complex/technical concept that would be easy for others to understand using a fable/toy story."
---

I want to understand a concept.

Please explain it by writing a fable — an indirect, narrative version of the concept. 

The story should embody the concept completely without naming it directly. 
Ideally, the reader should only start to realize what the concept actually is near the end of the story.

After the fable, add a short explanation that names the concept clearly 
and connects it back to the key moments in the story.
